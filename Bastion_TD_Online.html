HOW TO PLAY

Placing towers. Press UNITS to open the roster, pick a tower, then click an open tile on the map. While placing, valid tiles glow green and invalid ones glow red.
High ground. Tiles marked with ▲ are elevated bluffs — only the Longshot Nest can be built there. No other tower can, and the Longshot Nest can't be built anywhere else.
Blocked tiles. Rocky/cracked tiles can't be built on by anything.
Inspecting towers. Click a placed tower to see its live range, DPS, fire rate and special ability, plus Upgrade and Sell. At level 3 each tower unlocks unique specialization branches.
Weather. Changes between waves. Rain buffs Storm Coil; Snow slows ground foes; Fog cuts range; Heatwave buffs Flame; Thunderstorm strikes enemies with lightning; High Wind slows projectiles & speeds flyers; Nightfall raises enemy armor but buffs Archer/Sniper.
Boss rewards. When a boss dies the game pauses and you pick 1 of 3 random cards (Common → Legendary). Choices include lives, gold, global tower buffs, and tower-specific mastery.
Ultimates. Each tower charges an ultimate over time (faster during waves). Select a tower and press ACTIVATE when ready — Volley, Earthshaker, Blizzard, Chain Storm, Firestorm, and more. Great for bosses.
Flying enemies. Bats, Wyverns, Airships and the Sky Dragon ignore the path and fly straight to the exit. Only Archer, Longshot Nest, and Storm Coil can hit them. Cannon and Mortar are ground-only.
The Shop. Spend gold on permanent run-wide upgrades: bonus lives, gold income, fire rate, and unlocking new tower types.
Campaign. Five worlds of five maps. Clear maps in order to unlock the next. Each run reshuffles path, obstacles, weather, and elites. World hazards: Desert sandstorms, Peak ice, Infernal lava, Shadow portals.
Waves. Press SEND WAVE to start the next wave. Campaign maps have 8–12 waves (boss maps longer). Endless continues forever.
